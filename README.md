Advanced Platformer: Part 2

Welcome to the second installment of the Advanced Platformer series! This project features smooth gravity physics, custom level design, and more than 700 blocks of Scratch code built from the ground up.

🔄 What's New? (Loads Have Changed!)

The engine and aesthetics have been completely overhauled to bring you a more immersive experience:
• Dynamic Backgrounds: Added a custom background featuring color-changing effects that shift in respect to player movement.
• Modernized Art Direction: Revised the entire look and appeal of the platforms using the Scratch Pen tool, replacing generic blocks with sleek, modern designs.
• Themed Player Sprite: Recolored the player character to seamlessly match the levels' color palettes.
• Enhanced Physics & Limits: Implemented structural boundaries to control and refine the player's movement.
• Moving Platforms: Introduced moving platforms to elevate the puzzle-platforming challenge!
• New Hazards: Added spikes and lava pools for environmental danger.
• Interactive Elements: Fully integrated collectibles, doors, keys, and an atmosphere packed with immersive sound effects and SFX.

🎮 How to Play

• Movement & Jumping: Use your Arrow Keys to navigate through the levels and jump across platforms.
• Main Objective: Collect all the scattered apples and collectibles to clear your path.
• The Lock & Key: Find and pick up the hidden key to unlock doors and pass safely through restricted platforms.
• Survival: Dodge the hazardous spikes and lava to make it through alive!

🛠️ Built With
